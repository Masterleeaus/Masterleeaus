<h1 align="center">Jason Lee</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Environmental chemist · Independent technologist · Systems builder</strong></p>

I build software around a practical question: how can intelligent systems help people do complex work while keeping decisions understandable, accountable, and under human control?

My background is in environmental chemistry. Today, much of my work is focused on **Titan Zero**—an AI-first operating platform for field-service businesses—and on research and engineering tools that make evidence, context, and authority explicit.

## Selected projects

- **[Titan Zero Field Service Workforce](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/Titan-Zero-Field-Service-Workforce)** — A TypeScript business platform for field-service operations, combining customer and job workflows with a coordinated AI workforce, offline capabilities, and governed execution.
- **[Climate Crew](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/Climate-crew)** — A climate and environmental research platform designed around independent investigation, source provenance, competing hypotheses, and human validation.
- **[Titan Zero Interaction Engine](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/Interaction-engine)** — A workflow runtime connecting chat, voice, mobile, desktop, and API interactions to structured, policy-controlled business actions.
- **[Titan Developer Workforce Extension](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/Developer-Workforce-Extension-)** — A browser-based engineering workforce project exploring specialist agent profiles, orchestration, authority boundaries, and verification.
- **[WorkCore Extension Suite](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/workcore-extensions)** — An extension architecture that separates business domains behind explicit package ownership and integration contracts.
- **[Titan Design System](https://github.com/Masterleeaus/Titan-themes)** — Shared interface foundations and industry-specific themes for Titan products.

These projects are at different stages of development. Their READMEs describe each repository’s current scope and status.

## Principles I work by

- Evidence and traceable sources should matter more than consensus.
- Recommendations, authorization, execution, and verification are separate steps.
- Offline operation and recovery belong in the system design from the start.
- Software should connect to useful existing tools where appropriate.
- Claims about a system should match what its code and tests demonstrate.
